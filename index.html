Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

LiveOrder System ("we", "our", or "us") operates the LiveOrder application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

When you use LiveOrder, we may collect the following information:

  • Facebook Page information and Page Access Token (to send messages on your behalf)
  • Customer names and Facebook User IDs (PSID) from your Facebook Live comments
  • Order information including product codes and quantities
  • Basic account information you provide when setting up the app

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and manage orders placed during your Facebook Live sessions
  • Send automated private messages to customers confirming their orders
  • Monitor and display inventory levels in real time
  • Generate order reports and sales summaries for your business

3. Facebook Data and Messenger Platform

LiveOrder integrates with the Facebook Messenger Platform. We access your Facebook Page data and send messages through the official Meta Graph API. We use the pages_messaging permission solely to send order confirmation messages to customers who interact with your Facebook Live sessions.

We do not sell, share, or use Facebook user data for advertising or any purpose beyond the core functionality of processing live selling orders.

4. Data Storage and Security

Customer data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain order data only as long as necessary to provide our service.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data or your customers' data with any third parties except as required by law or as necessary to operate our service (e.g., hosting providers).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Disconnect your Facebook Page from our app at any time

7. Cookies

LiveOrder may use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us
